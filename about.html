About this project

Made by students who had the same questions.

Project Fundamentals is a Project Work resource built by four Hwa Chong Institution students for Sec 1–4 students — because the money knowledge you actually need day to day doesn't show up in any subject.

Why we built it

Many teenagers lack a clear understanding of how to manage their pocket money. Purchases happen without hesitation, saving feels abstract, and investing sounds like something adults do later. Without guidance, that gap doesn't close on its own — it just becomes an adult habit.

We wanted to build something that treats students as capable of understanding real financial concepts, rather than reducing everything to "spend less." That means explaining why overspending happens, what the real options for growing money are, and where the genuine dangers sit.

What our research found

Before building anything, we surveyed 57 Sec 1–4 students to check whether the problem we assumed existed actually did. It did.

  • 96.5% don't think school teaches enough financial literacy
  • 63.2% have never learned about investing before
  • 89.5% have encountered a suspicious 'get rich quick' offer online
  • 78.9% don't know how bank systems work

Based on a needs analysis survey of 57 Sec 1–4 students conducted as part of this project. Respondents skewed towards Sec 2.

How this resource is organised

Our research pointed to three areas where the gap was widest. Each has its own guide.

  • Spending Psychology & Clickbait

    The saving side — why money leaves your account faster than you intend, and how marketing and scams share a playbook.

  • Investing & Growth

    The growth side — what your options actually are, sorted by how much risk each one carries.

  • Banking & Digital Payments

    The spending side — how banks make money, and why tap-to-pay quietly changes your behaviour.
